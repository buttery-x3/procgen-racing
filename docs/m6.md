> uses its own branch from `development`, then is merged and pushed to remote
> `development` on completion for testing. Releases to `main` require an explicit

Implemented 26 October 2026 on `codex/m6-car-generation`, for integration and
testing on `development`. The M0–M5 reference cars and saved setups still work.
M6 adds generated vehicles to the existing four-contact driving model and keeps
the M3 proving circuit, M4 generator and M5 constructed venues available.

## Build and inspect a car

Open **Car workshop** from the header or navigation rail. Choose a seed, family,
performance band and supported engine/drivetrain layout. **Preview this seed**
commits seed edits; a changed seed disables applying/exporting the stale preview.
**Shuffle unlocked** changes the independent seed streams while respecting the
mechanics, proportions and appearance locks. Explicit dimension/section overrides
are retained by the proportions lock; explicit paint/trim overrides by appearance.

| Family              | Available layouts  | Recognizable features                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Touring hatch       | FF, front AWD, FR  | Short overhangs, tall cabin, hatch rear             |
| Grand touring coupe | FR, front AWD      | Long bonnet, two-door proportions                   |
| Sport sedan         | FR, FF, front AWD  | Longer cabin and separate boot                      |
| Mid-engine sport    | MR, mid AWD        | Forward cabin, short nose, rear engine deck         |
| Road hypercar       | MR, mid AWD        | Low, wide body and broad rear tyres                 |
| Rear-engine coupe   | RR                 | Rear-biased mass and engine vents                   |
| All-wheel sport     | front AWD, mid AWD | Compact performance sedan proportions               |
| GT endurance        | FR, MR             | Broad racing coupe, splitter and wing               |
| Endurance prototype | MR, mid AWD        | Low body, narrow canopy, covered wheels             |
| Open-wheel formula  | MR                 | Exposed tyres, nose, sidepods, helmet and wishbones |
| Stock car           | FR                 | Long, broad enclosed body and stock-car silhouette  |

These are fictional archetypes, with Road/Sport/Race parameter bands. They are
not manufacturer replicas or championship rulebooks. Supported layout options
are constrained per family rather than allowing every incompatible combination.

Expand **Dimensions & packaging** for wheelbase, independent front/rear tracks,
overhangs, tyre widths/radius, body/cabin height, cabin position/length and nominal
floor clearance. Body sections expose nose width, rear width, shoulder height
and roof width. Geometry, wheel locations and mass properties use the same resolved
definition. The formula's displayed body width is its central shell; exposed tyres
and sidepods extend beyond it.

Orbit/zoom the preview and use **Packaging overlay** to inspect the engine,
centre of mass and axle/wheelbase guides. Mass, estimated power, power/tonne,
front/rear balance, dimensions and COM height appear beside construction checks.
The preview renders on edits/orbit/resize rather than adding another driving loop.
**Appearance & seed streams** exposes paint, stripes, a visual rear wing and each
stream seed. Wing appearance does not change aero; tune aero explicitly.

Every preview must pass six construction checks and an isolated physical check
before **Apply** or **Export preview** becomes available. Construction checks cover
axle/cabin packaging, tyre clearance, component mass/COM, positive inertia, static
support/floor clearance and body proportions. The physical worker runs the same
120 Hz simulation as driving, settles for three seconds, then runs a 12-second
launch/brake test. It checks finite state, completed launch/braking, axle support
within 2.5 percentage points of the requested balance and total weight within
2.5%, grounded fraction above 97%, and bounded peak load/attitude. Results are
cached by mechanics fingerprint; seed edits invalidate pending results.

**Apply car & reset** rebuilds the vehicle on the current test and retains the
circuit. **Apply without reset** appears when mechanics are unchanged. Cosmetic
edits retain custom powertrain, suspension and chassis tuning; changing dimensions
carries explicit tune overrides where compatible. Changing family, layout,
performance band or mechanics stream establishes the new archetype's base tune.

## Mass, collision and wheel physics

`CarRecipe` v1 stores intent and independent streams. `CarDefinition` v1 stores
the resolved dimensions, sampled body sections, appearance and component boxes.
Components include structure/body panels, engine/transmission, cockpit/seats,
fuel and four wheel assemblies. Weighted component centres give the COM; box
inertias plus the parallel-axis theorem give the full symmetric inertia tensor.
Principal-axis decomposition supplies Rapier's moments and inertia-frame rotation.
The workbench's mass/balance edits rescale and redistribute component mass before
recomputing the tensor, rather than adding a second lump of chassis mass.

Generated cars use a massless base collider plus a cabin hull, or a formula roll
hoop; explicit component properties supply the rigid body's mass. Tyre radius,
axle tracks, mount height, visual hubs, contact origins, footprint probes and wheel
rotational inertia follow the resolved dimensions. The sectioned body has closed,
outward-wound surfaces and swept wheel-arch openings. Formula wishbones follow
the visual hubs, but are illustrative rather than physical linkages. The M1–M5
reference-car path retains its original dimensions and inertia.

This remains a semi-arcade, single rigid chassis with four suspension contacts.
Wheel assemblies contribute to chassis mass; independent unsprung bodies,
true linkage kinematics, tyre casing/heat/wear, damage and calibrated real-car
performance are outside M6. Broad family support does not imply equal suitability
for every circuit, extreme tune or driving style.

## Tune, save and compare

The five driving inspector tabs retain live diagrams and tyre telemetry. Use
**All settings** to expose the complete controls and per-wheel suspension spring,
damping and ride-offset overrides. **Link axle edits** preserves the front/rear
ratio while adjusting a paired setting. Numeric inputs and sliders share units,
and each field has a reset and a changed indicator against its saved reference
or generated factory setup. Derived frequencies, floor height and power/weight
update alongside them.

Mass, balance and chassis dimensions remain staged until **Apply chassis changes**;
**Discard changes** drops the draft. Drafts survive tab switches, and live tuning
does not leak staged chassis values into the simulation. Save/open-car/open-garage
actions require the pending draft to be resolved. Other settings apply live.
**Undo/redo** retains 50 complete setup snapshots and coalesces a slider gesture;
Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Y work outside editable fields.

Open **Garage / Compare** to save named complete car/setup snapshots. Save as new,
duplicate, rename, replace selected, load/reset, export selected/export garage,
and file/pasted JSON imports are supported. Imports become new entries, leaving
existing entries intact. The garage holds up to 24 browser-local setups; export
a backup for long-term storage or sharing. Failed persistence rolls back the
in-memory library to the last durable version.

Choose A and B independently from saved or working setups. A table highlights
differences; three cancellable worker tests run from the same settled spawn,
surface, inputs and fixed timestep without replacing the working car:

| Test                | Scripted conditions                                                        |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Launch & braking    | Isolated flat asphalt, 65% throttle for 4.5 s, then full brake; 12 s total |
| Steering response   | Same flat fixture, fixed throttle, sine steering after 3 s; 12 s total     |
| Suspension settling | Level pad, 0.6 m drop and no driving input; 6 s total                      |

Inspect speed, road-relative pitch/roll, yaw, inputs, individual loads, travel,
slip and RPM. Scrub both runs together; metrics include peak speed/attitude,
braking distance and 0–60 only if reached. These are matched diagnostic inputs,
not full-throttle performance figures. Export comparison JSON or save/overlay
recordings. Changing slots/scenario invalidates stale results. Manual recordings
may have different inputs even when captured on the same circuit.

## Recorder, controls and storage

**Expand response trace** opens the 120 Hz recorder. It captures a 20-second ring
of actual simulation steps, independently of the slower display refresh. Freeze,
change channel, scrub, name, save, export and reload runs. Frozen recordings remain
labelled separately from the live chassis diagrams. Recent history retains at most
six recordings within a 2 MB JSON budget, evicting older runs as needed; exported
JSON keeps full samples and is the archive format.

**Input settings** provides physical-key rebinding, duplicate-binding feedback,
standard gamepad button mapping, steering axis/dead zone/curve/response rate, a
curve plot and a live gamepad preview. Fields/dialogs suppress driving input.
Standard gamepad D-pad focus navigation, A activation, B close and focused
select/slider adjustment are implemented. Physical gamepad hardware has not been
available for verification; keyboard workflows and pure input transforms are tested.

| Browser key / export           | Version and contents                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `procgen-racing:setup:v3`      | Header quick save; reads v2/v1 when v3 is absent, preserving older keys |
| `procgen-racing:garage:v1`     | Named complete snapshots, stable IDs and timestamps                     |
| `procgen-racing:runs:v1`       | Recent recordings tagged with `m6-1` physics and 120 Hz samples         |
| `procgen-racing:input:v1`      | Validated keyboard/gamepad preferences                                  |
| `procgen-racing/setup` v3      | One car plus complete tune; legacy v1/v2 setup imports migrate          |
| `procgen-racing/garage` v1     | Complete garage export                                                  |
| `procgen-racing/comparison` v1 | Two complete recorded runs and their setups/metrics                     |

Current-format imports validate numeric bounds, enums, car construction and nested
versions. Unknown versions are rejected rather than silently loading a different
car. Exported definitions include resolved components, so reproducing a car does
not depend solely on future seed algorithms. Browser data is local to its origin;
there is no account sync. M8 timing/lap validity and ghost compatibility remain
future work.

## Verification and reproducibility

```sh
npm test -- --maxWorkers=2
npm run build
npm run qualify:m6 -- --count=1000 --driving
```

The [M6 qualification report](research/m6-qualification.json) records the exact
source/lockfile hash, hardware, sampled recipes, limits and measurements. The
campaign checks 1,000 seeds for each of 11 families, all three performance bands
and supported layouts; 550 sampled meshes; 33 physical acceptance cases; and
22 complete laps, every family on a dressed touring and GP venue. The lap driver
uses a conservative 0.55g reference with 16/20 m/s speed caps. This tests ordinary
surface/contact compatibility, not racing pace or every possible seed/tune.

The delivered campaign passed **11,000/11,000 constructions**, all **550 sampled
meshes**, **33/33 physical acceptance cases** and **22/22 full laps**, with zero
failures. Source/lockfile hash: **`19a00e579b4cc656`**. It took 165.0 s on the
Ryzen 5 5600X / 32 GiB development desktop, overlapping the regression suite.
Every lap retained four contacts; the largest total load was 1.188 times static
weight and the largest centreline error was 0.599 m. These are automated test
measurements, not browser latency or GPU frame-rate claims.

The final regression suite passes **188/188 tests across 13 files**; strict
TypeScript checking and the production build pass.

The automated regression suite adds determinism and stream independence, custom
tune preservation, geometry winding/finite vertices, COM/tensor reconstruction,
all-family equilibrium and inverted cabin support, corner tuning, storage/migration,
history, controls, ordered traces and repeatable comparisons. Browser QA covers
seed changes, rejected dimensions/imports, locks, apply, tune drafts across tabs,
undo/redo, cosmetic retention, library saves, A/B execution/scrub/export, recording
reload and input rebinding. The original saved reference setup is preserved.

Reports from M4/M5 retain their original hashes and measurement scope. Desktop
1080p/60 fps GPU certification remains separate from deterministic physics tests.
The production build still reports large Three.js/Rapier bundles; comparison and
car-acceptance workers load only when used. M7 supplies remaining circuit families
and scale; M8 supplies proper sessions, laps/ghosts and the complete presentation
and performance pass.
